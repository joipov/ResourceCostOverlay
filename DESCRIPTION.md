# Resource Cost on Icons

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**The mana, rage, energy, or focus a spell costs, written on the button.** Cooldown Manager spell bars and buff bars show it. Action bars stay clean unless you turn them on.

The number is the cost, not a percent, until you ask for a percent of your maximum. Colors can follow the resource: blue for mana, red for rage, orange for focus, yellow for energy. Otherwise the text stays the color you pick.

The minimap button is off until you turn it on. `/rco` opens settings. So does the addon compartment.
